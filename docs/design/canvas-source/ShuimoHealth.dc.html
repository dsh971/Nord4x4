Before your first row

A quick health check.

Based on the PAR-Q+. Your answers stay on this phone. Over 45 and not used to hard exercise, pregnant, or unwell today? Talk to a doctor first.

Has a doctor said you have a heart condition or high blood pressure?
Do you feel chest pain at rest, in daily life, or when you're active?
Do you lose balance from dizziness, or have you lost consciousness in the last 12 months?
Answer no if dizziness came from over-breathing, including during hard exercise.
Has a doctor diagnosed another long-term medical condition?
Do you take prescribed medicine for a long-term condition?
Some medicines change heart rate. A yes switches coaching to effort.
Do you have, or have had in the last 12 months, a bone, joint or muscle problem that more activity could make worse?
Answer no if it doesn't limit you now.
Has a doctor said you should only exercise under medical supervision?

Nor4x4 isn't medical advice. A yes to any of the first three pauses all rowing until a doctor clears you; other yeses keep pushes locked. Change answers any time in Settings.
