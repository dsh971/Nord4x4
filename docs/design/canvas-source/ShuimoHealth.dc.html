Before your first row

Row hard, row safely.

Pushes go close to your max heart rate.

Talk to a doctor first if you have a heart condition, chest pain, dizziness or fainting, are pregnant, or are over 45 and new to hard exercise.

Chest pain, dizziness or unusual breathlessness? Stop and rest.

Do you take medicine that affects your heart rate?
Beta blockers and some other medicines change heart rate. A yes coaches every row by effort instead of bpm. Ask your doctor about heart-rate targets.

Nor4x4 isn't medical advice. Nothing is locked; change your answer any time in Settings.
