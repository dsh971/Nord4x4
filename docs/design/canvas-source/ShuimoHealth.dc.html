Before your first push

A quick health check.

Pushes take you close to your max heart rate. Your answers stay on this phone.

Has a doctor said you have a heart condition or high blood pressure?
Do you get chest pain at rest, in daily life, or when you exercise?
Have you lost balance from dizziness or lost consciousness in the last year?
Do you have another long-term medical condition?
Do you take medicine for a long-term condition?
Could exercise make a bone, joint or muscle problem worse?
Has a doctor said you should only exercise under supervision?

Nor4x4 isn't medical advice. A "yes" keeps pushes locked until you've spoken to a doctor; easy rows stay open. Change your answers any time in Settings.
